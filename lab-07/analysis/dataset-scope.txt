EtherNet/IP:

was present and no packets were decoded as ENIP/CIP. Therefore this
traffic is not used as EtherNet/IP protocol evidence.

Supplemental public training captures will be used only for DNP3 and
EtherNet/IP evidence and will be identified separately in the report.
